# 📜 Complete List of All 20 Vedic Chandas

Vedic metres (*chandas*) are structured based on the number of syllables per **pāda** (line).  
They form the rhythmic foundation of Vedic hymns.

---

## 🔹 A. 7 Primary (Main) Vedic Chandas

These are the core and most frequently used Vedic metres.

1. **Gāyatrī** – 8 syllables  
2. **Uṣṇik** – 8 + 8 + 12 syllables (variable)  
3. **Anuṣṭubh** – 8 syllables  
4. **Bṛhatī** – 9 syllables  
5. **Paṅkti** – 10 syllables  
6. **Triṣṭubh** – 11 syllables  
7. **Jagatī** – 12 syllables  

---

## 🔹 B. 13 Secondary / Derived Vedic Chandas

These metres are expansions, extensions, or variations of the primary forms.

8. **Atijagatī** – 13 syllables  
9. **Śakvarī** – 14 syllables  
10. **Atiśakvarī** – 15 syllables  
11. **Atyaṣṭi** – extended Anuṣṭubh  
12. **Atyanuṣṭubh** – long Anuṣṭubh  
13. **Dvipadā Vṛttā** – 2-line metre  
14. **Dvipadā Virāj** – Virāj split into two lines  
15. **Virāj** – mixed-syllable metre  
16. **Aparavaktra** – rare traditional variant  
17. **Vipulā** – extended Anuṣṭubh variant  
18. **Śatobṛhatī** – expanded Bṛhatī  
19. **Madhu** – Vedic recitation pattern  
20. **Pragāthā** – mixed Triṣṭubh–Jagatī pattern
