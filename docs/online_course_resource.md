​[Page 2]
​In this Lesson (Asmin Pāṭhe)
• ​Division of Meters (Chandas – Prabhedāḥ)
• ​Syllabic Meter (Akṣara-Chandas) - Quantitative Meter (Mātrā-Chandas)
• ​Divisions of Syllabic Meter (Akṣara-Chandasaḥ Prabhedāḥ)

​[Page 3]
​Division of Meters (Chandasāṃ Vibhāgaḥ)

​[Page 4]

# Vṛttam (Syllabic Meter) is divided into:
• ​Equal Meters (Samavṛttāni)
• ​Semi-Equal Meters (Ardhasama Vṛttāni)
• ​Unequal Meters (Viṣama Vṛttāni)

​[Page 5]
​Summary of the Lesson (Pāṭhasya Sāraḥ)
• ​Meter (Chandas) is of two types – Vṛttam (Syllabic) and Jātiḥ (Quantitative).
• ​Vṛttam = Syllabic Meter (Akṣaracchandaḥ), Jātiḥ = Quantitative Meter (Mātrācchandaḥ).
• ​Vṛttam is of three types – Equal (Samam), Semi-Equal (Ardhasamam), Unequal (Viṣamam).
• ​Practice (Abhyāsaḥ) - Syllable Counting (Akṣara-gaṇanā), Measure Counting (Mātrā-gaṇanā).

[Page 3] Anuṣṭup
It is not a single Vṛtta (meter based on syllable count), but a Jāti (class/category) of meters.

[Page 4]
# Categories (Jātayaḥ) of Symmetrical Meters (Samavṛttānām):
Uktā, Atyuktā, Madhyā, Pratiṣṭhā, and Supūrvikā.
Gāyatrī, Uṣṇig, Anuṣṭup, and Bṛhatī, and Paṅkti.
Triṣṭup, and Jagatī, and also Atijagatī is accepted.
Śakvarī, and then Atipūrvā, Aṣṭī, and Atyaṣṭī are remembered.
Dhṛtiśca, and Atidhṛtiścaiva.

[Page 5] Anuṣṭup
The Anuṣṭup meter consists of four quarters (Pādas).
Each quarter has eight syllables (Pratipadaṁ 8 Akṣarāṇi).

[Page 6] Anuṣṭup
# Rule for the 5th, 6th, and 7th Syllables (Pañcamāṣṭamā Akṣarāṇi):
The 5th syllable is Light (Laghu) in all four quarters.
The 6th syllable is Heavy (Guru) in all four quarters.
The 7th syllable is Heavy (Guru) in the 1st and 3rd quarters.
The 7th syllable is Light (Laghu) in the 2nd and 4th quarters.

[Page 7] Anuṣṭup
# Śloka Definition (Lakṣaṇam):
"Pañcamaṁ laghu sarvatra, saptamaṁ dvicatuḥpadōḥ |
Gurū ṣaṣṭhaṁ ca vijñēyaṁ, śēṣēṣvanyaniyamō na hi ||"
# Translation:
The fifth [syllable] is light everywhere, and the seventh [is light] in the second and fourth feet (quarters).
The sixth [syllable] is to be known as heavy; and for the rest [of the syllables], there is no other fixed rule.

[Page 8] Anuṣṭup
